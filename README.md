# eCoins Estate demo

Concept demo of a South African crypto wallet and trading app.

- `index.html`: the demo account. You get R100 000 in demo funds and live prices (Binance USDT pairs converted at the live USD/ZAR rate, with a simulation if they can't be reached). Market, limit and stop-limit orders work, as do the wallet, history, a guided tour and support chat. State is saved in the browser's localStorage.
- `about.html`: the concept, money flow, what users said in reviews, and the roadmap.

No real money, accounts or addresses. Add `?notour` to the URL to skip the welcome screen.
