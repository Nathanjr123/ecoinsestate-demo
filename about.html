ecoinsestate.com · South Africa

Buy, hold and send crypto in rands, with a real person one tap away.

eCoins Estate is a crypto wallet and trading app for South Africans. It gets you from sign-up to your first R100 of Bitcoin in about five minutes, and it is built around the problems users report most in today's apps: slow support, account lockouts and costly sending mistakes.

The idea

A local crypto app that treats trust as the product

South Africa already has capable exchanges. User reviews show the gaps are in the experience around the trade: reaching support, getting back into a locked account, understanding fees, and sending without mistakes. eCoins Estate competes on those.

For

First-time and everyday buyers

People who want R200 a month in Bitcoin and don't want an order book. Pro mode is there for active traders who want one.

Promise

Rands in, crypto out, no surprises

Every screen shows values in ZAR. Fees appear before you confirm. Withdrawals go to the bank account in your own name.

Edge

Human support and safe sending

Live chat with named agents and visible reply times. The send flow catches missing tags, wrong networks and first sends to new addresses.

How we earn

Revenue model (draft)

Instant buy/sell fee of about 0.75%. Pro mode maker/taker fees of about 0.1–0.2%. A flat network fee on crypto withdrawals. Rand deposits by EFT are free. These numbers are placeholders to test against competitors.

What must be true

Licensing comes first

To operate we need an FSCA FSP licence (or to start as a juristic representative under a licensed firm), FIC registration as an accountable institution, Travel Rule compliance, and written approval from PayFast and our bank.

How it works

Where the money and the coins go

Customer rands sit in a separate client account at our bank, never in the company's operating account. Our internal ledger records who owns what. Trades are filled by a licensed liquidity partner, and crypto is held by a custody provider using MPC wallets, with most funds in cold storage.

flowchart LR
  U[Customer's bank account] -- "EFT / Instant EFT / card" --> T[Segregated client account]
  T --> L[(eCoins Estate ledger)]
  L -- "buy / sell orders" --> LP[Liquidity partner]
  LP -- "crypto settles" --> C[Custody provider: MPC hot wallet and cold storage]
  C --> L
  L -- "send crypto + Travel Rule data" --> X[External wallet or exchange]
  L -- "withdraw rands" --> U
  K[KYC provider: SA ID + selfie] -.-> L
  A[AML monitoring and FIC reporting] -.-> L
  1. Sign up and verify (FICA)Mobile OTP, 13-digit SA ID checked against Home Affairs through the KYC provider, then a liveness selfie. Level 1 is unlocked in minutes.
  2. Deposit randsEach customer gets a unique EFT reference. The bank feed matches deposits to accounts automatically. Instant EFT arrives in seconds.
  3. BuyWe get a quote from the liquidity partner, add our fee, and lock the price for 15 seconds. When the customer confirms, the ledger updates straight away and settlement with the partner happens in batches.
  4. HoldCoins sit with the custody provider. The app shows a live value in rands and the customer's profit or loss based on their actual purchase prices.
  5. Send or withdrawAddress checks, Travel Rule details, and a cooling-off period for new addresses. Rand withdrawals only go to a verified bank account in the customer's own name.
  6. Tax timeOne tap produces a capital gains statement for the SARS tax year (March to February).

Roadmap

From demo to launch

Licensing sets the pace. We build the product alongside it but can't take customer money until it's in place.

Phase 0 · now

Validate and get permission

  • Collect cofounder and tester feedback on this demo
  • Ask PayFast and the bank in writing whether they allow crypto
  • Hire a compliance consultant, and choose between a full FSP licence and starting as a juristic representative
  • Register with the FIC on goAML and draft the RMCP
Phase 1

Choose partners

  • Liquidity partner (a local exchange B2B API)
  • Custody provider (MPC) or a custody arrangement through the partner
  • KYC provider with SA ID and liveness checks
  • Segregated client account at the bank
Phase 2

Build the first version

  • Sign-up and verification, EFT deposits, buy and sell, wallet, send, withdraw
  • Ledger, admin console, AML monitoring
  • Live support chat
Phase 3

Closed beta, then launch

  • About 100 invited users with low limits
  • Security audit and penetration test
  • Public launch once the licence is confirmed
